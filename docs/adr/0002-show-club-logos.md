# Show club logos

We show each club's real logo, taken from its league's official site, instead of neutral monograms. Logos make the site recognisable and look finished, which matters for a portfolio piece; the trade-off is that logos are trademarks we have no licence for. We accept that for a non-commercial fan project, credit the source, and would switch to monograms if a club or league objects. Player photos are a separate question and are not covered by this decision.
