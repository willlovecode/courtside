# Courtside

The language of professional indoor volleyball competitions as Courtside presents them: who plays whom, in which competition, and who moves where.

## Competitions

**League**:
A country's top-division competition, such as SuperLega or PlusLiga. The body that runs it is an attribute of the League, not a separate concept.
_Avoid_: Competition, division, organiser (as a synonym)

**Season**:
One edition of a League, written as `2026-27`.
_Avoid_: Year, edition

**Phase**:
A part of a Season with its own format, such as the regular season, the playoffs or placement rounds.
_Avoid_: Stage, round (for this meaning)

**Round**:
One set of Matches within a Phase that belong together in the schedule.
_Avoid_: Matchday, Spieltag, gameweek

**Match**:
One game between two Teams, decided over up to five sets.
_Avoid_: Game, fixture

**Standing**:
A Team's position and record within a Phase.
_Avoid_: Table row, ranking

## Clubs and players

**Club**:
The lasting volleyball organisation, which keeps its identity and logo across Seasons and sponsor names.
_Avoid_: Team (for this meaning), franchise

**Team**:
A Club's squad in one Season, carrying the name the League uses for it that Season, sponsor included.
_Avoid_: Club (for this meaning), squad

**Player**:
One person who plays for Teams, recognised as the same person across Leagues and Seasons. When identity is uncertain, two records stay two Players.
_Avoid_: Athlete

**Roster**:
The list of Players registered for a Team.
_Avoid_: Squad list, lineup

## Movement

**Transfer**:
A Player appearing on a Team of a different Club than before, either between Seasons or during one. Loans count as Transfers.
_Avoid_: Move, signing, switch

**Announced transfer**:
A Transfer reported by a Club or League before it shows in any Roster.
_Avoid_: Rumour, done deal
